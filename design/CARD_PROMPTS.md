# Card art prompt pack

Every card is one image. Generate them in the Higgsfield **UI**, save with the exact
filename listed, and drop them all into `app/public/art/`.

- **Model:** GPT Image 2
- **Aspect ratio:** 3:4  (portrait — it fills the card frame)
- **Quality:** high · **Resolution:** 1k

**The background must be flat pure magenta (#FF00FF)** — not a gradient, not a glow.
The app cuts that colour out automatically, so one image becomes both the card
portrait *and* the fighter standing on the battlefield. Magenta is used because it
appears in none of these palettes, so it can never be mistaken for part of a
character.

Full body in frame with a little margin on every side, nothing clipped, head up.

## How to use this file

Paste the STYLE BLOCK, then the character line for the card you are making. The style
block is byte-identical every time — that is what keeps 64 cards looking like one set.
Changing a single word of it between cards is how a set stops matching.

## STYLE BLOCK — paste this first, unchanged, every single time

```
polished low-poly 3D cartoon character card art in the Clash Royale visual language, chunky rounded chibi proportions with an oversized head and small body, soft toy-like shading with gentle rim light, thick dark outline, saturated candy colours that pop, clean readable silhouette, full body facing camera in a confident three-quarter hero pose, centred and filling the frame, isolated on a completely flat solid pure magenta #FF00FF background with no gradient and no shadow on the background, no text, no letters, no words, no writing, no typography
```

Then append: `Character design: <the line below>. <palette> palette. Flat pure magenta #FF00FF background.`

## Checklist per image

- Head in the upper half, full body visible, nothing clipped at the edges
- The real logo/symbol is legible on the chest, shield or held object
- No text or lettering anywhere (the card frame draws the ticker itself)
- Background is a soft radial glow, not a scene
